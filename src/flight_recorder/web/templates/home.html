How it works

  1. Record. Each decision enters through one collector with the evidence it could see and the exact rules it ran.
  2. Preserve. That context is kept exactly as it was at the decision boundary. Later evidence never rewrites it.
  3. Replay. The same preserved context is evaluated under other rules, and the two results are compared side by side.
