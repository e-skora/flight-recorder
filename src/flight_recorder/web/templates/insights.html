Insights

How to read this page

  • The data is synthetic: every account, vendor-like event, decision and outcome on this site is simulated.
  • Every rate is an observed rate over the decisions recorded by the ledger cutoff this page reads. The comparisons are descriptive, not causal: no cause and no effect is claimed.
  • Counterfactual replay results are never recorded and are in no number on this page; there is no simulation view.
  • The signals and the workflow comparison are demonstration checks named by the synthetic dataset's manifest, not findings. This page shows observed numbers only and states no expectation about them.
